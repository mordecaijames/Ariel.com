Privacy Policy.

Last Updated August 2026

Ariel Agricultural Innovations ("Ariel," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website and our WhatsApp, phone, and email contact channels, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit the contact form on this website name, phone number, email address, and message content
  • Message us via WhatsApp, phone, or email directly
  • Request a site visit, consultation, or quote for any of our services

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as financial account details, national identification numbers, or health data through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries about our greenhouse, poultry, consultancy, seed and seedling, mechanization, aquaculture, soil, or irrigation services
  • To schedule site visits, consultations, and service delivery
  • To send updates related to an active enquiry or engagement
  • To improve our website and understand which services visitors are most interested in

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp & Third-Party Messaging

When you contact us via the WhatsApp button on this website, your message is handled through WhatsApp's own platform and is subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy in addition to this one. We use WhatsApp only to communicate directly with you regarding your enquiry.

4. Cookies & Website Analytics

This website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand visitor traffic and improve site performance. These do not identify you personally. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to affiliated service providers, social media platforms, or mapping services (such as embedded Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites — please review their respective policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and contact information only for as long as necessary to respond to your request, deliver an engaged service, or comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, where we are not legally required to retain it

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and agricultural enquiries and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at contact@arielagric.com, by phone at 0906 066 9964 or 0809 378 0074, or in person at 24/25 Kaduna Township Stadium, Adjacent Kaduna State Secretariat, Kaduna.
