Who We Are
Ariel Agricultural Innovations is an internationally acclaimed one-stop place for agricultural services a conglomerate of providers and equipment specialists affiliated with partners within and outside Nigeria. Our goal is simple and permanent: safe, affordable food, everywhere.

We operate a full, integrated farm, and we build integrated farms for our clients with practical, on-field training that transfers knowledge alongside infrastructure.
