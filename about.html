The team building Ariel, seed to harvest.

Six specialists a geophysicist, a chemical engineer, an agricultural researcher, a technologist, an accountant and a pilot running one integrated farm system out of Kaduna.

Our Story
Ariel Agricultural Innovations is an internationally acclaimed one-stop place for agricultural services. We are affiliated with major agricultural service providers within and outside the country, and we provide practical, on-field training on our own farm and on our clients' farms at an agreed fee. We operate a full, integrated farm, and we establish integrated farms for our clients, with a track record of delivering profitability from the first year of operation.

A.A.I is a conglomerate of agricultural services and equipment providers. Our goal is to provide safe and affordable food all around the world.

Leadership

The people behind the terrain.

Every discipline a modern integrated farm needs, held by one management team.

Opeyemi Bukola Oyewole

MRes, BSc Geophysicist & Surveyor

A highly experienced geophysicist and surveyor with a proven record in project management. Holds a Master of Research (MRes) in Marine Geology & Geophysics from the University of Southampton, UK, and a BSc in Geology from Ogun State University. Leads geophysical survey planning for data acquisition and crop specialisation.

Fanda Zogwia

MEng, BEng Chemical Engineer

A determined chemical engineer in the production of agro-chemicals, with over five years of experience. Holds a Master's and first degree in Chemical Engineering, both from the University of Aberdeen, UK. Specialises in weed control and herbicide science.

James Bello

PhD, MAgric, BEng Agricultural Researcher

A seasoned agricultural researcher who has worked extensively on irrigation and organic constituents for improving soil nutrients. Holds his doctorate and master's from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and his first degree from the University of Maiduguri. Specialises in crop nutrients, soil improvement and crop production.

Muhammed Abudullhi Matazu

ICT Agricultural Technology

A renowned expert in combining his passion for technology with the agro-business, and has constantly proven himself to clients. Holds a diploma in ICT from the University of Science & Technology, Omdurman, Sudan. Specialises in agricultural technological research and improvement.

John Musa

BSc, ACA Finance

A detail-oriented professional with experience in auditing, financial reporting, taxation and procurement. Holds a first degree in Economics from Kaduna State University. Specialises in procurement of goods and services.

Captain Sani Abdulkadre

Aviation

A trained pilot with over 10,000 flight hours. Earned his private and commercial pilot licences at a flying training service in Midrand, South Africa, and has since flown Nigerian airspace supporting Ariel's aerial farming methods.
