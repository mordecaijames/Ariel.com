Eight disciplines, one farm system.

From greenhouse steel to borehole water every service is built to connect, so a client scales from seed to harvest without switching partners.

01 Greenhouse Services Structures, low-cost builds, agronomics and materials
  • Installation of greenhouse structures 192sqm, 320sqm, family & choice sizes
  • Low-cost greenhouse builds using Melina, bamboo and timber
  • Greenhouse agronomics services
  • Supply of greenhouse seeds and materials
02 Consultancy Services Field science for small and large-scale operations
  • Pest control & soil enrichment programs
  • Crop and geographical survey specialisation
  • Marketing of agricultural crops
  • Hybrid farming and post-harvest handling
  • Poultry pen construction
  • Aerial methods of farming control
  • Seasonal seminars for small-scale farmers, largely women and youth, on post-harvest challenges
03 Poultry Services 98% success from pen to point-of-lay
  • Battery cages for broilers and layers
  • Point of Lay (POL) & day-old chicks from accredited hatcheries
  • Local, Noiler and affordable day-old chicks
  • Poultry pen construction
  • Drinkers, feeders, transfer cages, sprayers & chemicals
04 Seed & Seedling Breeding Foundational seed for grains, fruit and root crops
  • Exotic vegetables tomato, pepper, cucumber bred for Savannah climate
  • Improved root crops ginger and cassava
  • Rice, upland & lowland Faro and NERICA varieties
  • Maize, sorghum and wheat seed
  • Seedlings mango, cashew, cocoa, guava, dwarf pawpaw, passion fruit, avocado, strawberry, raspberry, grape, apple
05 Mechanization Affordable machinery for every farm scale
  • Precision refurbishing of tractors & farm implements
  • Fairly-used tractors for small-scale farmers
  • Complete new tractor kits for large-scale farms
  • Poultry feedmill mixers 1, 1.5, 2 tonne capacity
  • Galvanized fish roasters electric & charcoal
  • Local threshers built and foreign threshers sourced
06 Aquaculture Hatchery to harvest, pond to plate
  • Practical training hatchery through to harvesting
  • Supply of juveniles and movable, adjustable ponds
  • Construction of earthen and concrete ponds
  • Water-loving crop integration lowland rice, vegetables
07 Soil Services Diagnosis before intervention
  • Soil testing and analysis
  • Soil treatment and recommendations
  • Soil supplements for optimum yield
08 Irrigation Water security, on demand, all year
  • Industrial community-scale boreholes
  • Family-size & hand-drill boreholes
  • Greenhouse irrigation system installation
  • Open-field irrigation orchards & vegetable gardens
  • Sales of open-field & greenhouse irrigation systems
